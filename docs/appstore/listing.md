## English (U.S.) — primary

**Name** (30):
Rinse: Ad Blocker for Safari

**Subtitle** (30):

**Promotional text** (170):

**Keywords** (100, comma-separated, no spaces after commas):

**Description** (4000):

Rinse removes ads in Safari — from videos and from every website — on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

ADS GONE FROM YOUTUBE
Rinse for YouTube removes ads from the video player before they load. If one still slips through, it's skipped out of sight in a fraction of a second. Want videos to keep playing when you lock your iPhone or switch apps? Turn on background playback in the Rinse app.

A CLEANER WEB
Three content blockers carry about 143,000 rules from EasyList, EasyPrivacy and RU AdList, plus Rinse's own:
• Rinse Ads blocks ad networks.
• Rinse Privacy blocks trackers and analytics.
• Rinse Page Cleaner hides leftover banners and empty ad slots.

OFF WHERE YOU NEED IT
A site acting up, or one you want to support? Turn Rinse off there with one tap from Safari's extensions menu. Turn it back on just as easily, or manage the list in the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Safari applies the blocking rules itself, so Rinse never sees the pages you visit.
• No account, no analytics, no ads, no network requests of its own.
• Rinse collects no data at all.

FAST AND LIGHT
Blocking happens inside Safari's engine. Nothing extra runs on each page, so pages often load faster and use less data.

ONE PURCHASE, EVERY DEVICE
Buy once and use Rinse on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Filter lists are updated with each app update.

OPEN SOURCE
Rinse's code is public on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 license.

Setting up takes a minute: open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions (on Mac: Safari → Settings → Extensions) and turn on the four Rinse extensions. The app walks you through it.

Rinse works in Safari. It isn't affiliated with YouTube or Google.

First release.
